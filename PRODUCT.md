# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individual buyers of handmade model ships (collectors, gift buyers, people decorating a home or office). They browse the catalogue, compare models by category, size and SKU, then phone or write to the shop to ask for a price, because every product currently shows "Liên hệ" instead of a price. Vietnamese-language audience, on both desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Hoa Tay Việt (hoatayviet.net) is a workshop making handcrafted model ships ("Xưởng Sản Xuất Thuyền Mỹ Nghệ"). The site shows the range and gets visitors to contact the shop. Success is a visitor who finds a model they like and calls or emails. There is no online checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no backend, no database. Pages are generated by `scripts/build_site.py` from `_raw/products.json` and `_raw/home_sections.json`; `site/assets/css/style.css` and `site/assets/js/main.js` are hand-edited.
- 32 products in 7 categories: Tàu Du Lịch – Du Thuyền, Tàu Chở Hàng, Thuyền Buồm, Thuyền (Dùng đi dạo), Du Thuyền Hiện Đại, Nội Thất, Sản Phẩm Khác.
- Existing features: category mega menu, client-side search (`tim-kiem.html`), product pages with gallery, static pages (giới thiệu, liên hệ, hướng dẫn, tin tức, giỏ hàng, tài khoản). Cart and account are stubs and store nothing.
- Contact: phone 0908 062 685, email hoatayviet.modelship@gmail.com.
- Scope of this redesign (confirmed): home page and category pages, starting from `site/danh-muc/du-thuyen-hien-dai/index.html`. Product pages are not named in scope.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep all product content: names, SKUs, sizes, images, categories, menu labels and contact details.
- Keep the existing colour palette: blue `#0056A8` / `#003EAA`, red-orange `#E3411F`, CTA orange `#EF4A0E`, accent `#FFEBA4`, navy footer `#001A38` (values in `docs/research/DESIGN_TOKENS.md`). Reuse these; do not introduce a new palette.
- The earlier goal of a pixel-accurate clone of hoatayviet.net no longer binds. The old look is evidence of what to leave behind, and the measured values in README and `docs/research/` are reference only.
- The user wants a minimal, modern look. They explicitly rejected nested cards and gradients. Recorded as stated; the visual direction is decided in the design step.

## Evidence on Hand

- 32 products with names, SKUs, sizes and 217 images in `site/assets/img/` (many are GIFs mirrored from the original CDN).
- No prices, testimonials, customer logos, reviews or press. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

- The models are the content; photos of the ships lead, the interface stays out of the way.
- The route to contacting the shop (phone and email) is always within reach, since that is how a sale happens.
- Every product stays findable by category and by search.
- Content comes from the data files; design changes go in the generator and CSS, not into generated HTML.
