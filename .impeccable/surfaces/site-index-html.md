# Home + category pages

Mode: Persuade (catalogue home) / category listings. Visitor: individual model-ship buyer who phones the shop. Constraints: keep palette and all product content; flat colour only; no nested cards; no gradients.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The ships are the only decoration. A white, measured catalogue where the photos carry the colour and the interface is hairlines and type; it refuses the dated portal layout (top bar, mega menu, slider, boxed cards, cross-link noise).

OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink #111/#333 text, brand blue #0056A8 for links and primary action, navy #001A38 footer, orange #EF4A0E reserved for the phone/contact action only. Hairline rules rgba(0,0,0,.1) instead of boxes. Roboto body, Roboto Condensed bold headings. No shadows, no gradients, 2px radius max. Images sit directly on the page, no frames.

STORY: Visitor understands this is a workshop making handmade model ships, sees real ships at once, finds their category in one row, and calls 0908 062 685.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 72px single header bar (logo left, six nav links, phone button right, search icon). Below, a left-aligned condensed heading at ~64px "Mô hình tàu thuyền thủ công", one deck line, then two actions: filled blue "Xem sản phẩm", outlined "Gọi 0908 062 685". Three hero photos in a row at the fold, peeking into the viewport. Then a single hairline row of the seven categories as text links.

FORM: Catalogue index with a type-led opening; seed: none (pinned) "tối giản hiện đại, bảng màu dùng lại màu hiện có, loại bỏ card lồng nhau và gradient".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
